## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated recommendation: Next.js App Router with TypeScript and React, using React Three Fiber and Drei for interactive 3D, GSAP and `@gsap/react` for interface and scroll choreography, and `gltfjsx` as a build-time GLTF/GLB optimization and component-generation tool. Three.js remains the underlying rendering engine. Interactive 3D surfaces should be client-only, route- or section-scoped, and loaded progressively so server-rendered content remains fast and indexable.

The deployment target is still undecided.

## Users

The primary audience is manufacturing leaders, plant and production engineers, and procurement teams evaluating a custom industrial-automation partner.

Job applicants are a secondary audience. Career pages should attract strong engineering talent by showing the cutting-edge mechanical, controls, robotics, and automation work being done at VEEMAP.

## Product Purpose

The website presents VEEMAP Technologies' engineering capabilities and proof of delivery, then turns interest from prospective clients into qualified leads. Its primary conversion is a requirements enquiry that captures enough background about a prospect's manufacturing need for VEEMAP to prepare before responding.

Success means generating credible, relevant project enquiries rather than maximizing undifferentiated form submissions.

## Positioning

VEEMAP Technologies is an Indian engineering company that designs and builds custom high-speed production assembly systems, ranging from semi-automatic stations to fully automatic lines and complete manufacturing plants.

Its defensible position combines indigenous engineering, high-speed and precision automation, flexible multi-model systems, complete-plant capability, and an Industry 4.0 layer spanning controls, traceability, monitoring, and analytics. The source record includes 236 delivered projects, 55 clients across India and overseas, and named machines and lines with measurable production outcomes.

## Operating Context

Prospective clients arrive with a manufacturing requirement, process constraint, target output, quality or inspection need, product variant, or plant-scale automation challenge. They need to evaluate VEEMAP's sector experience, engineering depth, delivery range, and evidence from comparable machines before sharing their requirements.

VEEMAP serves Automotive, EV Solutions, Electronics, Consumer Goods, and Pharmaceutical/Medical manufacturing. Its delivery scope covers consultation and proof of concept through design, manufacturing, assembly, trials, production testing, commissioning, and connected-data systems.

The secondary careers journey should connect open roles to the real engineering work, learning opportunities, and technology exposure available at VEEMAP.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with engineering proof: show real systems, processes, outcomes, and constraints before broad marketing claims.
2. Qualify interest without creating friction: capture the project background VEEMAP needs while keeping the enquiry path clear and credible.
3. Make technical ambition useful: motion and 3D should explain mechanisms, scale, precision, or capability rather than act as decoration.
4. Preserve access to the core story: content, navigation, and lead capture must remain fast and usable on mobile, lower-powered devices, and reduced-motion settings.
5. Attract engineers through the work: careers content should demonstrate the problems, technologies, learning, and responsibility behind VEEMAP's opportunities.
