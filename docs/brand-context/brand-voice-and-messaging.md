## Rebuilt web-deck section labels / voice pattern

The rebuilt web presentation (`PPT/company-web-presentation.html`) uses a consistent short-statement, cinematic voice distinct from the live website's longer paragraph copy. Representative section eyebrows/labels found across the deck: "01 / PROMISE", "01 / COMPANY", "01 / PHILOSOPHY", "01 / JOURNEY", "02 / MARKET REACH", "03 / PROOF", "03 / DIFFERENTIATION", "03 / ENGINEERING IN MOTION", "04 / MEDICAL AUTOMATION" (section divider), "05 / ELECTRONICS", "06 / DIGITAL LAYER", "07 / ORGANIZATION", "08 / WAY FORWARD". Case-study slides use a "CASE 0N / [SECTOR]" label pattern (e.g. "CASE 01 / EV", "CASE 02 / AUTOMOTIVE", "CASE 04 / ELECTRICAL", "CASE 05 / CONSUMER GOODS"). Source: `PPT/company-web-presentation.html` throughout.

Note: this labeling system and the short declarative headline style ("Safety. Efficiency. Engineered.", "From one station to the complete plant.", "Scale the system — not just the machine.") is the rebuild project's own editorial voice, not verbatim source-deck copy — it is included here as a documented pattern for future copywriting reference, not as an established VEEMAP brand voice guideline. The `PPT/README.md` audit table confirms this deck was "substantially redesigned" from the original slides.

## Tone pattern observations

- **Live website copy**: first-person plural ("we"), direct address to prospective customers ("you"/"your"), short marketing paragraphs emphasizing customization, quality, and partnership. Recurs across every page (Home, Capabilities, Case Study, Pharmaceutical, EV, Careers).
- **PPTX internal deck copy**: terser, data/spec-driven, uses internal shorthand and abbreviations (e.g. "assy" for assembly, "m/c" for machine, "CT" for cycle time, "ppm" for parts per minute).
- **Portfolio and News pages are currently Wix template placeholder text**, not real VEEMAP copy — flagged explicitly in source: `Website_data/veemap_context/08_PORTFOLIO.md` ("Welcome to my portfolio. Here you'll find a selection of my work...") and `Website_data/veemap_context/09_NEWS.md` ("Share your feature information here to attract new clients..."). Do not treat this placeholder text as brand voice reference.
