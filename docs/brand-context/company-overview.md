- **2017** — Company founded, team of 2, small-room factory.
- **2018** — Partnership with Keyence; Clutch assembly line for FCC. Sales 5 M INR, team 6.
- **2019** — R&D Lab established. Developed FA products: high-speed PPU, Delta Robot, Rotary Table. Sales 19 M INR, team 15, factory 450 m².
- **2020 (COVID)** — Developed prototypes: Ambu Bag assembly, Ventilator bench. Also: ET tube assembly, Cuff & ET assembly, Pilot Check valve assembly. Sales dipped to 13 M INR, team 20.
- **2021** — High-Speed Line milestone; per the web deck's year attribution this covers "Asia's fastest Line" — Singolo Pump (150 ppm) and Cap Liner insertion (250 ppm). Sales 35 M INR, team 30, factory 1000 m² reached by 2022 per the table (source table places "1000 M2" under the 2022 column).

- **2023** — Medical Machines milestone. Sales 95 M INR, team 40.
- **2024** — High Speed Line milestone; per the web deck this year is where "Asia's fastest Singolo pump (170 ppm) line for RIEKE Vietnam" is placed. Sales 188 M INR, team 46.
- **2025** — "Smart Automation" milestone. Team 60, Sales 500 M INR (internal-only figures — do not publish, see note above).
